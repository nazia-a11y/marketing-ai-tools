How this works, and what it cannot tell you
  1. The target is split into the revenue marketing must source, then divided by the deal size to give the deals needed.
  2. Each conversion rate is applied in reverse to give opportunities, SQLs, MQLs and leads. The channel mix gives the average cost per lead and the budget.
  3. All of these numbers are calculated in your browser with fixed formulas. They are never produced by AI.
  4. Claude is only asked to review the finished figures, and is told to use nothing else. Reviews are kept for 24 hours, so asking again about the same figures is free.

This is a planning model, not a forecast. It assumes the rates hold steady across the year and every lead costs the channel average. Real funnels vary by segment, season and deal size, so use it to find the constraint and test what-if scenarios, then check the result against your own CRM data.
