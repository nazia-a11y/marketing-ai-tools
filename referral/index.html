How this works, and what it cannot tell you
  1. Each month, a share of your customers refer someone. The tool assumes each one refers one person, which is cautious, since a shared link often reaches more.
  2. A share of the people referred sign up, and a share of those become paying customers. If you sell through a sales team, it uses your own win rate, raised a little for referred leads, and assumes deals close about three months after sign-up.
  3. The programme starts small and builds to full strength over six months. New referred customers join your customer base and can refer others too.
  4. Cost per customer is the rewards paid plus the running cost, divided by the new customers. This is compared with what paid channels cost you to win the same number of customers.
  5. All numbers are calculated in your browser with fixed formulas. They are never produced by AI. Claude is only asked to review the finished figures, and reviews are kept for 24 hours, so asking again about the same figures is free.

Where the assumptions come from. Customers who refer each month: 2% to 5% early on, 10% to 15% for mature programmes (Cello customer data, 2026). Referred people who sign up: 15% to 25% when the invitation sits inside the product, under 5% when something is broken (Cello platform data). Referred free users who become paying: 8% to 12% (ProfitWell, quoted by GrowSurf). Referred customers cancel about 18% less often (Schmitt, Skiera and Van den Bulte, Journal of Marketing, 2011, a study of bank customers). Typical rewards: 5% to 20% of first-year value (Cello and Impact.com, quoted by GrowSurf). The uplift for referred sales leads is an assumption, as no reliable published figure exists.

This is a planning estimate, not a forecast. Results depend heavily on where the invitation appears, how much customers like the product and how easy it is to share. Use it to judge whether a programme is worth testing and which number to focus on, then measure the real rates once it is running.

Sources: Cello on sales-led referral programmes, Cello on referral conversion, GrowSurf SaaS referral statistics, Schmitt, Skiera and Van den Bulte (2011).
