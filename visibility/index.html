Buyers now ask AI assistants which providers to shortlist. This tool asks Claude five typical buyer questions about your category and shows how often your brand is named, compared with your competitors.

Run a check

It takes about a minute. Each run asks 20 questions in total.

Free runs are limited each day to keep costs down.
How this works, and what it cannot tell you
  1. Five buyer questions are built from your category and country, for example "What are the best CRM platforms in the United Kingdom?"
  2. Each question is asked three times with no web access, to show what the model already knows, and once with live web search, to show what it finds today.
  3. Brand names are counted with simple rules. AI is used only to spot other brands the answers name, so new competitors appear.
  4. Results are kept for 24 hours, so running the same check again is free.

Answers in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini change from person to person, with their history, settings, location and the model version, and can differ each time. This check asks neutral questions with no personal context, so treat it as an estimate and a trend to watch, not what any one buyer sees.
